## [Publishing][publishing]

This has been the first month without my bestseller. I had to take it down because of potential copyright issues. As fas as I can tell, all my other competitors are still up and doing pretty well. I think they are making around $600 in profit/month. Maybe **I was too cautious**. Only time will tell.

On the other hand, I have spent more money and **pushed hard my last book**, that I published in July. This is probably the first completely optimized listing I have, with full A+ content, video review, and video ads. The book has been still losing money (around $200) for all August. But now that it's ranking, I am optimizing ads and seeing the first break-even week coming. Maybe next week will be already in profit. I have also **optimized all my other listings**. Added full A+ content to all books, improved covers and descriptions where needed, and also published the hardcover version of some of my books. I think **I underestimate hardcovers**. For each of my books I sell one or two hardcovers per month. And it's just a no-brainer to spend 30 minutes making a hardcover version of the book. Most of the work is on the cover design and at this point I have become capable of doing it myself.

I **have published two books in August**. Currently still collecting ARC readers, and soon I will start running ads. One is a seasonal book, the other one is pretty much evergreen. I am going to run an aggressive ad strategy for those two, and let's hope things go well. How many books have I published this year so far? Only six, I thought more. By the end of the year, I would have published 9 books I think. Not too many, **my goal was 10 to 12 books by year-end**. I have probably overestimated my capabilities from one side. But from the other side, I have noticed that I can probably write and format around 2 books per month now. The problem is launching them with the **right timing** and finding the budget to launch them properly and push with ads.

Last but not least, I have created a Trello board to keep track of all my books progress, from idea to production to marketing. It's helping me a lot in finding where to focus my efforts, now that I have many books in the pipeline.

I am completely focused on Q4 right now. My next book is targeting Halloween. And then I have another one in the pipeline that will be perfect for Christmas. Plus, a lot of my books are giftable and should do really well during the gift season. The investment should pay off. How big?
