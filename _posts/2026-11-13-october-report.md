## [Publishing][publishing]

I launched two books in October. They are both Advent calendars. One is for the US market, the other is exclusively for the Italian market. The US market one has an interesting angle, but I think I made a crucial mistake with the title that might cost me a lot of money. In October, I spent $400 in ads for this title, and it's currently sitting at around 45k BSR.

The Italian book is similar to a mildly successful book that I published back in February. I published it in the end of October, so no ad spend yet. But in November I am spending a few hundred bucks because I really think it could get to top 300 BSR in Italy.

I recognize that made a few mistakes with these books, that I need to correct for the next ones.

- Launched too late. I should have launched maybe 1 month earlier. Now I need to spend aggressively on ads to make it rank and it's less than ideal. Next time I target a seasonal book I will launch 2 months before.
- I did not pay enough attention to the keywords I want to rank for in the title. For these type of books it's crucial to try to rank for as many KWs as possible.

But I also did a few things right

- I launched in the Italian market. I think this is easier to penetrate. Ad spend is 4-5x lower than in the US, and there is much less competition.
- I found a way to get reviews for the Italian market. If this works and those reviews come in (so far so good) this could solve my biggest pain point. I plan to publish much more in this market in the future.
